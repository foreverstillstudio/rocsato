Hillhurst Estate · The Acoustic Sanctum

The Recital Room

Some rooms hold sound. This one gives it back.

A private, high-ceilinged acoustic space in rural Pennsylvania with timber floors, historic plaster resonance, and a grand piano. We do not rent an empty venue by the hour—we welcome Visiting Artists into a sanctuary built for concentrated acoustic craft.
